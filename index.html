    What Lenium doesn't spell out

    The Rules page says a position may not exceed 5% of your balance and that the "combined value" of open positions may not exceed 10%. It doesn't say whether that's the price you paid, the current market value, or whether fees count. This checker uses the cost at entry (contracts × price), which is also the most a position can lose. Check Lenium's Rules page if you're near a cap.

    It also doesn't say whether an order over the caps is blocked or counted as a breach ("attempting to circumvent position size or exposure limits by any means" is a termination condition). Don't place one.

    How it's calculated

    Rules as read from lenium.capital on Oct 23, 2026 (Rules, Pricing and Tools pages). Evaluation accounts only; Instant and funded accounts have their own limits. Lenium's Rules page is the final word.
